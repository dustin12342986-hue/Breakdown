Crew work

    
One row per person per event. Leave rate blank to use the crew-rates file. Status: "done" means paid. Everything not "done" counts toward Open Contractor Obligations.

    

    

  

  

    
Paste a roster (CSV)

    
Header row: name,role,event,eventDate,units,rateType,rate,status. rateType is day or hour. Dates as YYYY-MM-DD.

    
    

  






  

    
Crew rates

    

    

  

  

    
Paste crew rates (CSV)

    
Header row: name,role,dayRate,hourlyRate,w9,profile,type. w9 and profile are yes or no. type is contractor or w2.

    
    

  






  

    
Fixed costs

    
Recurring bills that are not contractor pay. Due day is the day of the month. A due day past the end of a month uses that month's last day.

    

    

  

  

    
Paste fixed costs (CSV)

    
Header row: name,amount,dueDay

    
    

  






  

    
Events and tax set-aside

    
Set-aside is 25% of Event Net Revenue once payment is received. Net = revenue minus contractor pay for that event (from Crew work) minus direct costs. Blank direct costs makes the number provisional. Moving a set-aside into the Tax Bucket is a button, so nothing is counted twice.
